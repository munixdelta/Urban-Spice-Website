About Urban Spice

Passionate About Great Food & Exceptional Service

Urban Spice Events & Catering is a Bangalore-based catering service focused on bringing delicious food and dependable catering support to celebrations, gatherings and events.

We believe that great food is at the heart of every memorable event. Whether you're planning an intimate house gathering or a grand wedding celebration, our team is committed to delivering quality, freshness and professional service at every step — from planning to the final serving.

Our cuisine expertise spans across North Indian, South Indian, Chinese, Punjabi, Gujarati and more — ensuring every guest at your event finds something they love.
